About Us

Who we are:

At Lucky Shrub, we are more than just a landscaping company; we are a husband-and-wife team united by a shared, lifelong love for plants. Founded by Jason and Maria, our business is built on the belief that great outdoor spaces come from both artistic vision and dedicated care..


Maria ensures the heart of our operation thrives, managing the nursery and leading our marketing efforts to connect us with fellow plant enthusiasts.

Jason, our lead garden architect, designs and oversees every project, managing a talented team of landscapers to bring your dream garden to reality.

About Our Company:

Based in Tuscon, Arizona, Lucky Shrub is a medium-sized garden design firm that specializes in garden design and creation, maintenance and landscaping. The company also runs a small plant nursery that sells indoor and outdoor plants, making them a one-stop shop for clients to "transform any space into an oasis you can be proud of".

Together, we blend creativity with hands-on expertise to create gardens that are not only beautiful but also healthy and sustainable. From the first sketch to the final bloom, we are committed to bringing your outdoor vision to life with passion and precision.
